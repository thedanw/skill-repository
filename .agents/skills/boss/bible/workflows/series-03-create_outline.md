# Series Outline Creation Workflow

## Step 1: Contextual Grounding

1.  **Analyze Theological DNA**: Read the documents in `.knowledge/user-DNA` (specifically the Grace Dynamic) to ensure alignment.
2.  **Review Research**: Check `[SeriesPath]/00_Research/` (CoreResearch.md and Summary.md) for key motifs, historical context, and cultural bridges.

## Step 2: Proposed Outline (Strategy Phase)

Generate a high-level table for the requested number of weeks (or a suggested breakdown for a book/topic). Use the following format:

| Week  | Title          | Bible Story                 | Passage     | Application                   |
| :---- | :------------- | :-------------------------- | :---------- | :---------------------------- |
| **X** | [Punchy Title] | [Name of Narrative/Passage] | [Reference] | [Grace-driven Call to Action] |

**Requirements for Proposal:**

- **Titles**: Short, active, and focused on the "now."
- **Application**: Must be "Grace-Driven" (focus on what Christ has done to empower obedience).
- **Flow**: Ensure a logical thematic progression through the series.

---

## Step 3: Fleshing Out (Development Phase)

Once the user approves the high-level table, generate the detailed breakdown for each week following the **Series Formatting Guide**.

**Detailed Format Structure:**

```markdown
### Week X: [Title]

**Passage**: [Ref]
**Summary**:

[2-3 unbulleted lines providing thematic overview - NO BULLETS]

**[Subheading 1]:**

- X:X-X [verse reference with brief description]
- X:X-X [verse reference with brief description]

**[Subheading 2]:**

- X:X-X [verse reference with brief description]

**Key Verses**:

- X:X "[Exact quote from NLT]"
- X:X "[Exact quote from NLT]"
```

**Critical Principles for Drafting:**

1.  **Summary**: Always 2-3 lines of plain text. **Strictly no bullets** in the summary section.
2.  **Logic**: Subheadings should capture the "Movement" of the passage (Law to Gospel, or Problem to Solution).
3.  **Translations**: Use the **New Living Translation (NLT)** for all scripture quotes.
4.  **Citations**: Use `[CITE]` format for any insights drawn directly from specific research sources.
